# Paper Edge Tuning Report Design

This report follows the Data Analytics portable report contract: answer first, evidence second, caveats and next steps last.

- Audience: product and strategy stakeholders.
- Surface: narrative report with an 800px reading column.
- Visual language: neutral surfaces, compact metric cards, blue comparison charts, diverging time-block bars around zero, and direct labels/tooltips.
- Evidence hierarchy: baseline and selected exploratory policy metrics, daily stability, time-block diagnostics, then the complete candidate table.
- Critical semantic rule: green or positive values indicate retrospective simulation performance only; they do not imply a proven live edge.
